Our Journey

Started in Mysore with a vision to redefine luxury, Star Group Interiors has grown exponentially over 10+ years of experience. What began as a premium interior design studio has evolved into a full-spectrum firm specializing in exterior facades, turnkey execution, and custom chandelier manufacturing. Today, we proudly serve residential and commercial clients across Mysore, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi, and other major Indian cities, delivering timeless elegance and impeccable craftsmanship.
