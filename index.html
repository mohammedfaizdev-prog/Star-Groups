About Star Group Interiors

Star Group Interiors is a Mysore-based interior design company delivering premium residential and commercial interior solutions across Karnataka.

With over a decade of rich industry experience, we successfully deliver premium interior and exterior projects for commercial and residential clients all across India. Renowned for our meticulous craftsmanship and professional execution, we have earned distinct recognition for our specialized division in custom chandelier manufacturing. Our expertise bridges the gap between classic luxury and contemporary design, creating stunning, tailor-made lighting fixtures ranging from traditional, grand crystal chandeliers to sleek, minimalist modern designs.

Our interior design projects span across Mysore, including Vijay Nagar, Jayalakshmipuram, and the city center, as well as major cities across Karnataka and India.
